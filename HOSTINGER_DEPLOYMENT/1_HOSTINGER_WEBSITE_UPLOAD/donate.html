Kya UnivGeeks abhi bhi free rahega?+

Bilkul. Donation sirf platform ko zinda aur behtar rakhne ke liye hai — notes, PYQs aur formula sheets hamesha students ke liye free rahenge. Koi paywall nahi, koi ads nahi.

Mera paisa kahan lagega?+

Server & domain, naye notes/PYQs banane ka kaam (scan, solve, format), aur platform ki designing me. Har mahine ka usage breakdown hum apne social channels par share karte hain.

Bina donate kiye bhi support kar sakte hain?+

Haan! UnivGeeks ko apne doston, school group aur juniors ke saath share karna sabse bada support hai. Instagram par follow karke bhi humein motivate kar sakte ho.

Donation ka process kaisa hai?+

Payment gateway jaldi live ho raha hai. Us tak aap neeche wale form se apni salahakat bhej sakte ho — hum aapko UPI / payment link seedha bhej denge.
